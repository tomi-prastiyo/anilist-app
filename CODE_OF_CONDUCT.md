# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in **Mankai** a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic in issues, discussions, and code reviews.
- Credit upstream authors, data providers (**AniList**), and open-source inspirations (**AL-chan**).
- Keep feedback focused on technical merits and user experience.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, discriminatory remarks, or publishing private information.
- Submitting malicious code, spam PRs, or circumventing security/API rate limits.

Maintainers may close issues/PRs or restrict participation for behavior that violates these standards.
